# Lexivant Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Lexivant
**Etymology:** *lex* (Latin: law, rules) + *avant* (French: ahead, forward)
**Meaning:** "Ahead of every rule" — forward intelligence on AI governance
**Tagline:** Intelligence Before It's Law
**Sub-line:** The AI Governance Intelligence Platform

---

## Brand Story

Lexivant exists because AI governance doesn't wait. Across Africa, India, and the
Global South, regulators are writing the rules of tomorrow — today. Lexivant is the
early-warning intelligence system that turns regulatory noise into strategic clarity,
giving AI companies the knowledge they need before compliance becomes a crisis.

We watch. We analyse. We translate law into action.

## Logo

### Logomark (Hexagon + L)
The hexagon represents **interconnected regulatory borders** — six regions, one
intelligence platform. The angular "L" within is bold and directional, implying
forward motion. The diagonal accent slash implies speed and real-time awareness.

### Lockup Variants
- **Full horizontal** — `LEXI` (bold) + `VANT` (light) with tagline
- **Icon only** — hexagon mark for favicon, app icons, small placements
- **Reversed** — white on dark for sidebar / dark backgrounds

### Clear space
Minimum clear space = 1× the hex icon height on all sides.

### Don't
- Stretch or skew the logo
- Use on low-contrast backgrounds
- Recolour individual letterforms

## Voice & Tone

- **Authoritative** but never condescending
- **Global South perspective** — centred on Africa, India, emerging markets
- **Precise** — regulatory intelligence demands accuracy
- **Forward-looking** — we see what's coming, not just what's here

### Writing principles
1. Lead with insight, not description
2. Translate regulation into business impact
3. Name the markets (Lagos, Nairobi, Brussels — not "various regions")
4. Use active voice; regulations *require*, they don't *suggest*
